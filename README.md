# 🌿 Green Bridge
### A Farm-to-Consumer Marketplace for Mzansi, by Mzansi

> *"I grow food that feeds the city. But the city does not know my name. If they could see my farm and buy from me directly, everything changes."*
> — Hlumelo Bhayi, Small-Scale Farmer, Eastern Cape

---

## Overview

**Green Bridge** is a social impact eCommerce platform that directly connects South Africa's small-scale farmers with urban consumers — cutting out intermediaries, reducing food waste, and bringing transparency back to the food supply chain.

Small-scale farmers currently receive as little as 20–30 cents of every rand spent on fresh produce, with the rest absorbed by wholesalers, logistics operators, and supermarket chains. At the same time, urban consumers — particularly in townships and lower-income areas — face retail markups of 200–400% on fresh produce, with no visibility into where their food comes from or how it was grown.

Green Bridge repairs this broken system.

## Features

### 🌱 Farm Stories
A rich, human-centred narrative panel embedded on every product detail page. Unlike a standard product description, the Farm Story surfaces the farmer's identity, geography, growing philosophy, and personal narrative — transforming an anonymous commodity transaction into a relationship between a named producer and a conscious consumer.

Each Farm Story includes:
- Farmer photograph and name
- Farm location (province and district)
- First-person farmer narrative (50–120 words)
- Harvest timeline (planting date → estimated harvest → days since harvest)
- A direct **"Message the Farmer"** button via WhatsApp

### 🔔 Smart Surplus Alerts
An adaptive pricing and notification system that responds to fluctuations in product availability. When farmers flag excess stock, the system dynamically repositions it as a time-sensitive discounted deal — surfacing it to consumers before it goes to waste.

Key components:
- Automated discounted pricing with visual indicators
- Dedicated **Surplus Deals** section with urgency cues
- User notifications based on browsing and purchase history
- Low-stock labels and countdown timers to drive faster purchasing decisions

### 🛒 Standard eCommerce Functionality
- Product listings with category, location, price, and organic/surplus filters
- Shopping cart with quantity controls and promo code support
- Checkout with **Home Delivery** or **Community Collection Point** toggle
- Payment integration designed for PayFast, SnapScan, and EFT
- Order confirmation with WhatsApp tracking and farmer notification

## UX Design Approach

Green Bridge was designed for two distinct user archetypes with very different digital literacy levels:

** Hlumelo Bhayi — Small-Scale Farmer, Mthatha EC**
Low digital literacy, Samsung Galaxy A14, WhatsApp-first user. The farmer interface prioritises large tap targets, icon-driven navigation, minimal text input, and onboarding achievable in under 5 minutes.

** Aaliyah Wilson — Urban Consumer, Lavender Hill Cape Town**
High digital literacy, iPhone 16, frequent online shopper. The consumer interface features farmer profile photos, farm location context, surplus deal notifications, and a polished, frictionless checkout.

> A platform that works for a smallholder farmer in Mthatha on a mid-range smartphone will, by necessity, be fast, clear, and accessible for every user.
